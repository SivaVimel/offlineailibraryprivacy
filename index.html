Babel

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Applies to Babel for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0 and later

The short version: Babel collects nothing.

The AI that writes your books runs entirely on your device. Your ideas, your books and your settings never leave it, and we have no servers that could receive them. Babel makes no network connections at all.

Information we collect

None. We never receive any information from Babel: it does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There is no account or sign-in, and Babel contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs.

On the App Store, Babel's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

What stays on your device

To work, Babel keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

WhatWhy
Your books — the ideas you asked for, their titles, chapter plans and textSo your library and "Continue Reading" work, and nothing is lost if the app is closed
Where you are in each bookSo a book reopens at the line you left it
Your theme, text size and Discover suggestionsSo the app looks the way you left it

You can remove any book from your library at any time (press and hold its cover). Deleting Babel deletes all of this information.

How your books are written

Books are generated by an AI language model that is built into the app and runs on your device's own processor. What you type is used only to write your book, on your device. It is not sent to any server, used to train any model, or seen by anyone.

Sharing

Babel shares nothing on its own. If you choose Copy Text or Share in a book, the book's text goes to the clipboard or to the app or person you pick in the iOS share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy, not this one.

Backups

Like other app data, Babel's library can be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud Backup or a computer. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple; Babel has no access to them.

Permissions

Babel does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other protected information.

Children

Babel does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because books are written by AI from whatever the reader asks for, parents may wish to review the app's age rating on the App Store.

AI-generated content

Books written by Babel are generated by AI and may contain inaccuracies or invented details. They are not professional advice of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If Babel's handling of information ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. A change that means Babel would collect data would also be reflected in its App Store privacy label before it takes effect.
